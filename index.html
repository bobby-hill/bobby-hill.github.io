Your question is turned into a list of 384 numbers, called an embedding, by all-MiniLM-L6-v2, a small sentence-embedding model. Each part of this portfolio, along with a few example questions it answers, is embedded the same way.

The results are the parts whose embeddings point in the most similar direction to your question's, measured by cosine similarity. So a question can match without sharing any exact words.

The model runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
